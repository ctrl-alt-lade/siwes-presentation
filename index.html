Slide 04 • Focal Engineering Project

1-DOF PID Robotic Joint Implementation

Precision angular control architecture incorporating real-time telemetry and algorithmic disturbance rejection.

Hardware Subsystems Core Setup

Driven by an Arduino Uno (ATmega328P), interfacing a 5x4 Matrix Keypad for operator target inputs, an L298N Dual H-Bridge power stage, and a 12V DC Gearmotor with active inductive snubber protection.

Closed-Loop Feedback 2.75 pulses/deg

Incorporates a Hall Effect quadrature encoder measuring continuous displacement at 2.75 pulses/degree, enabling sub-degree angular telemetry and dynamic error correction: e(t) = Setpoint - Actual.

Software-Based Calibration Zero-Limit Hack

Replaced physical limit switches with an innovative firmware "Jog Mode" calibration routine, eliminating mechanical switch bounce, contact wear, and spatial assembly constraints.

Slide 06 • Diagnostic Engineering

Fault Diagnosis & Root Cause Remediation

Systematic troubleshooting protocols deployed to resolve critical electrical and mechanical failure modes.

CHALLENGE 01 Electrical Subsystem
Unidirectional Motor Fault (Blown BTS7960 Driver)

Observation: Actuator operated strictly clockwise; counter-clockwise switching triggered immediate voltage collapse and thermal runaway.

SOLUTION: Traced gate continuity with a digital multimeter, confirmed blown high-side MOSFET on the BTS7960 module, and safely swapped to an isolated L298N driver with flyback diode protection.
CHALLENGE 02 Mechanical Subsystem
High-Torque Mechanical Coupling Slip

Observation: Under rapid PID reversals, the motor D-shaft experienced rotational slip against the 3D-printed PLA joint bore, corrupting encoder telemetry.

SOLUTION: Fabricated a chemically hardened composite hub with reinforced grub-screw seating, eliminating rotational backlash and locking the shaft firmly under peak torque.
